About the Shuttle

Getting you where you need to be

Bringing your vehicle in for maintenance shouldn't mean rearranging your whole day. Our complimentary shuttle is available to every service customer, whether you're in for a quick oil change or a multi-day repair.

Tell your advisor you'd like a ride when you book or when you arrive. We'll drop you anywhere within the service area, and when the vehicle is ready we'll come back for you. If you've been in an accident and your vehicle is with us, the same goes: you can depend on us to get you where you need to go.

Good To Know

Shuttle service FAQ

Is the shuttle really free?

Yes. The shuttle is complimentary for all Golden Mile Chrysler service and parts-installation customers. There is no charge and no tipping expected.

Do I need to book the shuttle in advance?

We recommend requesting the shuttle when you book your service appointment, ideally 24 hours ahead. Walk-in requests are welcome and we'll accommodate them whenever we can, but seats go on a first-come, first-served basis.

How far will the shuttle take me?

Our standard service area is roughly a 10 km radius around the dealership, which covers most of Scarborough, East York and eastern North York. See the service area map above. If your destination is further out, call us and we'll see what we can do.

How long will I wait for a ride?

Shuttles depart regularly throughout the day. Because we group riders heading in the same direction, your trip may include one or two other stops. On busy mornings (7:30 to 9:00 AM) please allow a little extra time.

Can I bring my children or a pet?

Children are welcome. Please bring your own car seat or booster, as required by Ontario law, and let us know ahead of time so we can plan seating. Service animals are always welcome; for other pets, please call ahead.

What if I'm not ready when the shuttle comes to pick me up?

No problem. Your service advisor will coordinate a pick-up time that works for you. If your plans change, just call 647-288-5337 and we'll reschedule the return trip.

Is the shuttle wheelchair accessible?

Our shuttle can accommodate folding mobility aids. If you require a fully accessible vehicle, please let us know when booking and we will make alternate arrangements for you.

Can the shuttle pick me up from home to bring my vehicle in?

The shuttle runs from the dealership outward, so your vehicle needs to be dropped off first. For customers who can't make it in, ask your advisor about vehicle pick-up and delivery options.
